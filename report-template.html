Summary of the source document

What this report shows

All 106 studio profiles and every original profile field. Multiple cities named in the same profile get their own map markers but remain one table row. The selection is the document's selection — not a complete directory of all existing game studios. The table shows the full project field, even when several projects are named.

Currency and job security

The document states no date of collection. Its content has not been validated as current industry research. The four security classes are editorial, keyword-based summaries of the original text, not forecasts or credit ratings. The full original text appears in every profile. The "Independent / private" category may include minority stakes; the full investor field remains authoritative.

Job research

The search covered Technical Art (including shaders and artist pipelines), Technical Animation/Rigging, Art (artists, animation, VFX, and art direction), and Tools Engineering. Pure gameplay or rendering engineering roles without a tools focus are not included. Capture is title-based and may miss titles in other languages and dynamically loaded postings. Open applications alone are not confirmed vacancies. "No matching portal results" applies only to the queried source and the assigned cities; never to all career opportunities of a company.

For public job APIs, locations are assigned based on the city in the posting. Shared portals and teams may show the same position. Key figures count unique job URLs; each profile view names the actual posting location. Remote options and membership in individual teams must be checked in the posting. No automatic update on opening — external positions may have closed since.

Links and preview images

Website and career targets were assigned editorially; the check status appears in the profile. LinkedIn links are only marked as found on the website when the profile URL actually appeared on the researched page. Other LinkedIn targets are editorial, unconfirmed profile assignments. After relocations or closures they may be invalid. Where no dedicated careers page was found, the link leads to the studio website.

Available game preview images are embedded Steam store capsules of representative released titles; they do not necessarily illustrate the current project. Image and title link to the respective store page. All image rights belong to the respective rights holders. For some projects and pure support studios, no suitable preview images exist; this is indicated visibly. Country flags are embedded as small images from FlagCDN / Flagpedia for reliable offline display.

Map, filters, and colors

The map uses Natural Earth 1:110m (public domain), a simplified geographic representation with no statement on border claims. Locations are based on approximate city centers; for Re-Logic only the Indiana region. In densely covered regions, markers are spread apart for selection; fine connecting lines point to the original city center. Zooming reduces the geographic offset. Continent buttons only change the map section, not the filters. For a filtered continent list, use the continent filter. For multi-location profiles, country and continent filters are applied to the same location. Genre, engine, and investor colors show the first normalized category. Filters consider all assigned genres and engines as well as Tencent minority stakes explicitly mentioned in the investor field; unmentioned stakes are not added.

Legend buttons show or hide pins for their color category. All categories start visible. Selections are retained separately per color mode. Reset restores every category. These toggles affect only the map, not the directory or statistic cards.

One file, no runtime dependencies

The complete geometry, all data, available preview images, styles, and scripts are embedded in this HTML. Internet is only required for outgoing links. No analytics scripts, cookies, map APIs, or external fonts. "Reset" clears all filters; color selection and map view are preserved.
